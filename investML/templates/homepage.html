How InvestML Works

Five simple steps to intelligent, risk-aware investing

1

Create an Account

Sign up to create your InvestML account. Once registered, you can securely log in and log out at any time.

2

Create Your Portfolio

Build your portfolio to calculate your risk tolerance score. This score determines how much of your capital should be invested in stocks.

3

ML Stock Prediction

For each individual stock, InvestML provides a machine learning prediction indicating whether the stock is likely to grow or fall.

4

Smart Data-Driven Allocation

InvestML fetches real-time financial data from the Yahoo Finance (yfinance) API, including profit margins, and allocates your budget based on your risk profile.

5

ML-Enhanced Allocation

Finally, machine learning–based allocation is integrated with profit-margin allocation to generate an optimized, intelligent investment strategy.
